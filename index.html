Welcome to Aurelia

Sixty years of slow mornings.

Built in 1962 as a small family guesthouse, Aurelia has grown into a hotel of forty-two rooms without losing its calm. Linen curtains, stone floors and wide balconies face the water, and every room opens to fresh sea air.

We keep things simple: comfortable beds, honest food and people who remember your name.

Good to know

Before you arrive.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included in every room rate and served on the sea terrace from 7:00 to 11:00.

Can I check in early?

When your room is ready we are happy to give it to you early. You can always leave your bags with us and go to the pool.

Do you allow pets?

Small dogs are welcome in garden rooms for $20 per night. Please tell us when you book.

How do I cancel my booking?

Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before arrival. Sign in to your account to change or cancel a stay.
